How to Clean Your Waterproof Picnic Blanket

Your waterproof picnic blanket from PicnicPro USA is designed for adventure – from sandy beaches to grassy parks and damp forest floors. While built tough, keeping it clean is key to its longevity, hygiene, and ensuring it's always ready for your next outdoor escapade. A little care goes a long way!

General Cleaning: Spot Treatment for Minor Spills

For everyday spills, crumbs, or mud splatters, immediate spot treatment is your best friend. This prevents stains from setting in and keeps your blanket fresh between deeper cleans.

  • Act Quickly: The sooner you address a spill, the easier it is to remove.
  • Remove Solids: Gently scrape off any solid food bits or excess mud with a dull knife or spoon.
  • Damp Cloth & Mild Soap: Mix a small amount of mild dish soap or laundry detergent with warm water. Dampen a clean cloth (microfiber works great) with the solution.
  • Blot, Don't Rub: Gently blot the stained area. Rubbing can push the stain deeper into the fabric. Work from the outside of the stain inwards.
  • Rinse: Dampen another clean cloth with plain water and blot the area to remove any soap residue.
  • Air Dry: Allow the treated area to air dry completely before folding or storing.

Deep Cleaning: Machine Washing (If Applicable)

Some waterproof picnic blankets can be machine washed, but it's crucial to always check the manufacturer's care label first. Machine washing can sometimes be too harsh for the waterproof backing if not designed for it.

  • Check Label: Verify if your blanket is machine washable. If not, proceed to hand washing.
  • Pre-Treat Stains: Apply a stain remover to any stubborn spots before washing.
  • Gentle Cycle: Use a gentle or delicate cycle with cold water. Hot water can damage the waterproof coating.
  • Mild Detergent: Use a small amount of mild, non-bleach laundry detergent. Avoid fabric softeners as they can sometimes leave a residue that affects waterproofing.
  • Wash Alone or with Similar Items: To prevent damage and ensure a thorough wash.

Deep Cleaning: Hand Washing for Best Results

Hand washing is often the safest and most recommended method for most waterproof picnic blankets, especially if you're unsure about machine washing, as it preserves the integrity of the waterproof layer.

  1. Shake Off Debris: Take your blanket outside and give it a good shake to remove loose dirt, sand, and crumbs.
  2. Prepare a Basin: Fill a bathtub, large utility sink, or a clean large bucket with lukewarm water.
  3. Add Mild Soap: Add a small amount of mild laundry detergent or dish soap to the water and agitate to create some suds.
  4. Submerge & Soak: Place the blanket into the soapy water, ensuring it's fully submerged. Let it soak for 15-30 minutes to loosen dirt.
  5. Gently Scrub: Use a soft brush or sponge to gently scrub any stained areas or heavily soiled spots. Pay attention to the waterproof side and the fabric side.
  6. Rinse Thoroughly: Drain the soapy water. Refill the tub with clean, cool water and rinse the blanket thoroughly. You may need to do this a few times until all soap residue is gone and the water runs clear.
  7. Gently Squeeze: Carefully press out excess water. Avoid twisting or wringing too aggressively, as this can stress the fabric and waterproof layer.

Drying Your Waterproof Picnic Blanket

Proper drying is just as important as washing to prevent mildew and preserve the blanket's waterproof qualities. Never put a waterproof picnic blanket in a tumble dryer. High heat can melt or damage the waterproof backing.

  • Hang to Dry: The best method is to hang your blanket outdoors on a clothesline or over a sturdy railing. Ensure it's spread out fully to allow for maximum airflow.
  • Indoors Option: If drying indoors, hang it over a shower rod, a clothes horse, or several chairs, ensuring good ventilation. Place towels underneath to catch any drips.
  • Completely Dry: Make sure both sides of the blanket are absolutely dry before folding and storing. Even slight dampness can lead to mildew or odors.

Storage Tips for Longevity

Once clean and dry, proper storage helps maintain your blanket's condition and prevents damage.

  • Fold Neatly: Fold it according to its original creases if possible, or roll it tightly to save space.
  • Cool, Dry Place: Store your blanket in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures.
  • Breathable Storage: If possible, avoid sealing it in airtight plastic bags for extended periods, as this can trap moisture. A breathable storage bag is ideal.

By following these simple cleaning and care instructions, your PicnicPro USA waterproof picnic blanket will remain a reliable, comfortable, and hygienic companion for countless outdoor adventures for years to come!
