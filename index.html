Why You Need a specialized "Picnic Mat" (Not Just a Towel)

In the US, "Picnic Blankets" are a specific category. Unlike a regular throw, they feature:

  • Padding: Foam middle layer for comfort on rocky ground.
  • Portability: Folds up into a tote bag with a handle.
